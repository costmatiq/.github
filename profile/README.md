# CostmatiQ — One Connected Cost Intelligence Platform

**CostmatiQ Private Limited** ([https://costmatiq.com](https://costmatiq.com)) builds enterprise should-costing, 3D CAD/CAE manufacturability analysis, and multimodal AI sourcing software for industrial OEMs and Tier-1 suppliers.

---

## Core Engineering & Costing Architecture (7 Commodity Families · 100+ Process Engines)

| Engine | Domain | Key Capabilities |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **QCad** | 3D CAD & DFM | B-Rep STEP/IGES feature recognition, dimension extraction, wall-thickness & DFM checks |
| **QPlast** | Injection Moulding | Undercut detection, gate/sprue/runner design, cavity layout, 3D mould fill & cooling simulation |
| **QSheet** | Sheet Metal & Stamping | Unfolding, progressive strip layout, laser/stage nesting, 3D die visualization & draw-bead planning |
| **QCast** | Die & Sand Casting | Undercut detection, gate/runner/riser design, core extraction & solidification fill/cool analysis |
| **QForge** | Hot & Cold Forging | Forgeability assessment, preform & die sequence, flash allowance & press tonnage calculation |
| **QCam** | CNC Machining | Multi-axis toolpath simulation, stock generation, operation routing & cutting tool selection |
| **QPCB** | Electronics & PCBA | 3D PCB visualization, panelization, layer/drill stackup analysis, PCBA DFM & BOM costing |
| **QJoin** | Assembly & Welding | Virtual assembly sequence simulation, cell design, line balancing & MOST time-study analysis |
| **Quinn & Agent 5.0** | Multimodal AI Assistant | Voice & chat BOM assistant, 2D GD&T drawing extraction, quote-gap analysis & live negotiation levers |

## Intelligence Masters
- **RM Master:** Global raw-material grade, property, TDS, indexing, and CO2e alternative libraries.
- **Machine Master:** Global machine library with regional machine-hour rate breakups.
- **Supplier & Labour Masters:** Regional wage, overhead, markup norms, and HS-code **Duties & Tariffs**.
